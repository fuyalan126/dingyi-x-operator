## Reply Snippets

### What is this?

It is a reusable X operator skill that helps agents handle replies, posting, source adaptation, and visual planning with better safety defaults.

### Why not just use a generic writing agent?

Because X operations need workflow judgment: target selection, reputation guardrails, explicit confirmation, and better handling of live execution.

### Who is it for?

Founders, builders, operators, and creators who want help running X without sliding into low-signal automation.
